# BBRewrite Documentation

**BBRewrite** is an AI-powered writing assistant that rewrites text to match your personal tone, style, and preferences. This documentation provides a complete guide to using the service safely and effectively.

---

## 1. Overview

BBRewrite helps users:

* Rewrite text into their personal writing style
* Improve clarity, tone, and flow of writing
* Save time on editing

BBRewrite is operated by **Sikder LLC**. All references to “BBRewrite”, “we”, or “our” refer to Sikder LLC.

> Note: BBRewrite is an automated AI tool. Users are responsible for reviewing all generated content before use.

---

## 2. Getting Started

1. **Sign Up** – Create your account using your email.
2. **Submit a Style Example** – Enter the text you want rewritten or provide a text that represents your desired style. This lets the agent know how you want your output to sound.
3. **Choose a Plan** – Free or paid plans determine your daily rewrite limit and available features.
4. **Use the Rewrite Button** – When you open a page, a popup **Rewrite** button will appear. Click it, and your text will be rewritten in real time.

> Tip: Properly submitting your style text ensures the best rewrite results.

---

## 3. Supported Platforms

* **Web Application:** Accessible on modern browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari)
* **Browser Extension:** Provides the Rewrite button on supported sites

## 5. Usage Best Practices

* Provide clear input and/or style examples for accurate rewriting.
* Avoid submitting sensitive or confidential information.
* Always review output before sharing or publishing.

---

## 6. Troubleshooting

| Issue                            | Solution                                                                                       |
| -------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Rewrite does not trigger         | Check your daily limit; ensure you have remaining rewrites                                     |
| Browser extension not responding | Refresh your browser; reinstall the extension if needed                                        |
| Output seems off                 | Submit a clearer or more accurate style example; review output before use                      |
| Need very long text rewriting    | Currently not optimized for very long text; contact support via the submit form for assistance |
